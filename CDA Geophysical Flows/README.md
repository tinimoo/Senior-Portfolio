# CDA Introduction

Of all the projects in this portfolio, this is the most in line with my interests and career goals. I worked on this project as part of a Research Experience for Undergraduates at Mississippi State University, funded by the National Science Foundation. Our goal was to test the effects on of continuous data assimilation, a numerical method which incorporates real-world data into simulations, on the accuracy of a model for ocean currents. We were able to show that incorporating data from only one of the variables in the model would produce long-term stable and accurate results, in agreement with past results. I chose to include this project because it is the most advanced research I’ve yet performed, and incorporates all three of my top interests. This project gave me the opportunity to learn about models based on partial differential equations, the process of transferring these models to code, and the process associated with incorporating real-world data into an abstract mathematical model. This project demonstrates my ability to learn and implement graduate-level mathematics into useful results with useful real-world applications. It also demonstrates my proficiency with programming in Matlab, and especially with translating a mathematical model into working code. If I could do anything differently in this project, it would be to explore more of the mathematics behind the model I was mostly focused on programming and analyzing results, however the model itself was very interesting and I wish I had taken more time to study and understand it.
